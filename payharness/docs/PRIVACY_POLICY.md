# PayHarness Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 September 2026

This policy is a product draft and should be reviewed and approved by the service operator and qualified privacy/legal counsel before publication as a binding privacy notice.

## 1. Information we process

Depending on how the service is used, PayHarness may process:
- merchant account and contact information;
- merchant business/profile configuration;
- user and role information;
- API usage and request metadata;
- payment, payout, checkout-session, transaction, and webhook records;
- provider configuration and verification metadata;
- audit and security logs;
- support communications.

Provider credentials and other secrets are protected configuration and should not be included in ordinary logs.

## 2. Why information is used

Information may be used to:
- provide and secure the PayHarness service;
- authenticate users and authorize merchant operations;
- process and reconcile payment activity;
- deliver webhooks and operational notifications;
- monitor service health and security;
- investigate incidents and support requests;
- comply with legal or contractual obligations.

## 3. Data minimization

Integrations should send only metadata necessary for payment reconciliation and business operations. Avoid putting sensitive personal information into payment metadata unless it is required and appropriately protected.

## 4. Providers and service providers

Payment operations may involve third-party payment providers and infrastructure providers selected by the merchant or PayHarness. Information required to perform an enabled integration may be transmitted to the relevant provider.

## 5. Security

PayHarness uses authentication, authorization, encryption for protected provider credentials, signed webhooks, audit logging, rate limiting, dependency/security scanning, and production monitoring controls appropriate to the service.

No security control can eliminate every risk. Merchants remain responsible for securing their own systems and credentials.

## 6. Retention

Records are retained according to operational, contractual, reconciliation, security, and legal requirements. Retention periods should be defined by the service operator's approved data-retention schedule.

## 7. Rights and requests

Privacy requests should use the support channel provided in the merchant account. Requests may require identity and account verification before action is taken.

## 8. International processing

Infrastructure or payment providers may process information in countries other than the merchant's location, subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

## 9. Changes

This notice may be updated as the service, providers, or legal requirements change. Material updates should be communicated through the established merchant communication channel.
